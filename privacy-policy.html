Pixel Champs Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Pixel Champs respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the current Pixel Champs app collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

The current version of Pixel Champs may collect:

  • Email address: used to create and authenticate a player account.
  • User ID: used to associate your account and saved game progress with your player profile.
  • Product interaction: game progress and gameplay-related information such as experience, level, virtual coins, and best score, used to provide and sync game functionality.

How We Use Information

We use this information to authenticate players, provide account features, save and synchronize game progress, and operate and support Pixel Champs.

Service Providers

Pixel Champs uses Supabase for account authentication and cloud data storage. Information processed by Supabase is used to provide the services described above.

Advertising and Tracking

The current version of Pixel Champs does not use third-party advertising, advertising networks, or cross-app tracking of users.

Payments and Virtual Items

The current version of Pixel Champs does not process real-money payments inside the app. Virtual coins and other virtual game items have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money.

Data Retention and Deletion

Account and game data are retained as needed to provide the account and game features. If you want your account or associated data deleted, please contact the Pixel Champs developer through the support contact provided with the app's App Store listing.

Children

Pixel Champs is not intended to collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the game. Parents or guardians who have privacy questions may contact the developer through the support contact provided with the app's App Store listing.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Pixel Champs changes its data practices. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the support contact provided with the Pixel Champs App Store listing.
